Rhodium — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 August 2026. This is a starter policy for a pilot/hackathon deployment; review with counsel before commercial launch.

Rhodium ("we") helps merchants sell and collect payments through WhatsApp and keep sales records. This policy explains what we collect and why.

1. Information we collect

  • Merchant details you provide during onboarding: business name, phone number, and bank account details for payouts.
  • Buyer details: the phone number used to place an order, and order/receipt data.
  • Transaction data: products, orders, payments, and an append-only sales ledger.
  • Messages you send to our WhatsApp number, to operate the service.

2. How we use it

To provide the service: process orders, request and confirm payments, send receipts and notifications, and maintain your sales records. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Payments & no custody

Payments settle directly to the merchant — to their bank account (via a licensed payment processor) or their own crypto wallet. Rhodium never takes custody of funds.

4. Data handling & security

Personal and financial identifiers are encrypted at rest; access is limited to operating the service. We aim to handle data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

5. Third parties

We use WhatsApp/Meta to deliver messages, and payment processors (e.g. Paystack) and blockchain networks (e.g. Quai) to move funds. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies.

6. Retention & your rights

We keep records for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. You may request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us.

7. Contact

Questions or data requests: info@officialapps.com.
